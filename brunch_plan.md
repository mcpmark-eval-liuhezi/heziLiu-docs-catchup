## Main Dish: 手抓饼 (Hand-Pulled Pancake) — Difficulty: ★★ (2/5)

**Ingredients (per serving; scale up by 4 for our party):**
- 面粉 (all-purpose flour) — 200 g
- 开水 (boiling water) — 100 ml
- 冷水 (cold water) — 50 ml
- 食用油 (cooking oil) — 15 ml
- 盐 (salt) — 3 g
- 鸡蛋 (egg) — 1
- 生菜 (lettuce) — 30 g
- 火腿 (ham) — 30 g
- 芝士片 (cheese slice) — 1 slice

**Steps:**
1. Mix 200 g flour with 100 ml boiling water (half-hot-water dough), then add 50 ml cold water, 15 ml cooking oil, and 3 g salt. Knead into a smooth dough and rest.
2. Divide into portions, roll thin, and layer with oil and salt between sheets to create flaky layers.
3. Pan-fry in a little oil over medium heat until golden and crispy on both sides.
4. Optionally fry an egg on top and assemble with lettuce, ham, and a cheese slice inside the pancake.

**Tip:** For 4 people, multiply ingredients by 4 (≈800 g flour, 4 eggs, 120 g ham, 4 cheese slices, 120 g lettuce).

## Companion Dish: 茶叶蛋 (Tea Eggs) — Difficulty: ★★★ (3/5)

**Why this one:** A classic Chinese brunch item that can be made ahead on the weekend (the recipe notes it's perfect for weekend cooking and one batch feeds 2–3 people), it adds protein and a savory-sweet flavor that complements the savory hand-pulled pancake, and it pairs beautifully with the lettuce/ham/cheese fillings.

**Ingredients:**
- 鸡蛋 (eggs) — 400 g (~8 eggs)
- 八角 (star anise) — 4 g (~2 pieces)
- 香叶 (bay leaves) — 0.5–1 g (~2 pieces)
- 桂皮 (cinnamon bark) — 3 g (1 small piece)
- 茴香 (fennel) — 5 g
- 冰糖 (rock sugar) — 15 g
- 红茶 (black tea) — 20 g
- 生抽 (light soy sauce) — 15 g
- 老抽 (dark soy sauce) — 25 g
- 食盐 (salt) — 3 g

**Steps (summary):** Place eggs in a pot, cover with tea, star anise, bay leaves, cinnamon, fennel, rock sugar, and both soy sauces and salt. Bring to a boil, then simmer 20–30 minutes. Remove eggs, crack the shells, and let them soak in the broth overnight for maximum flavor. (Full recipe: dishes/breakfast/茶叶蛋.md in HowToCook)
